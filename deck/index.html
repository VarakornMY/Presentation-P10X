The joint initiative

P10X Academy × UTM for Orang Asli youth

A shared TVET initiative with four objectives for sustainable economic participation.

  1. 01

    Knowledge transfer

    Transfer relevant TVET Auto Aesthetics knowledge and technical skills to Orang Asli youth.

  2. 02

    Skills development

    Build practical competency through hands-on, industry-oriented training.

  3. 03

    Industry exposure

    Introduce participants to real industry practices, standards and working environments.

  4. 04

    Economic empowerment

    Create pathways towards employment, entrepreneurship and income generation.

UTM × P10X ACADEMY03 / 11

Programme strategy

A structured pathway from access to employment

  1. 01

    TVET awareness

    Develop community outreach with Orang Asli participants and families.

  2. 02

    SKM Level 2 entry

    Offer a structured skills qualification pathway for Orang Asli youth.

  3. 03

    Seven months of training

    Transfer practical skills through guided training and assessment.

  4. 04

    Job matching

    Connect participants with roles targeting RM2,000 in monthly basic salary.

  5. 05

    Income monitoring

    Follow participants’ employment and income over 6–12 months.

Certification depends on assessment; job offers and salaries depend on employer placement.
